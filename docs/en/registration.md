# Team registration

The **AgroTech Robotics Challenge** is held in two tracks:

- **Track 1** — Unitree Go2, autonomous scout
- **Track 2** — Unitree G1, agro sorter

A team chooses **one track** for the entire season. Team size is 2 to 4 people;
individual applications are allowed. Details are in the
[rules](rules.md#3-participants).

---

## Register

!!! info "Registration form"

    Fill out the Google Form — this is the primary way to submit an application:

    **[→ Open registration form](https://forms.gle/REPLACE_WITH_ORG_FORM){ .md-button .md-button--primary }**

    The organizing committee will replace the link with the live form before the
    season opens. If the button is not active yet, email

## What you will need to register

| Field | Example |
|------|--------|
| Team name | `AgroBots NU` |
| Institution | New Uzbekistan University |
| Track | Go2 or G1 |
| Captain | Full name, Telegram / email |
| Roster | Full names and roles of participants |
| Advisor | optional |

---

## After registration

1. Receive confirmation at the captain's email (within **[pending approval]** business days).
2. Complete [Quick start](quickstart.md) — Docker + first simulator run.
3. Study [learning materials](learn.md) and documentation for your [track](tracks/index.md).
4. Follow the calendar on the [home page](index.md) — stage dates are published with
   the approved rules.

!!! tip "First stage — no robot needed"

    A laptop with Docker is enough for the qualifier. Real robots are available in
    the NEWUU laboratory at the semifinal and final.
